# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are not
acceptable. Assume good faith while still addressing harmful impact.

Report conduct concerns privately to `conduct@contextstream.io`. Maintainers may
remove content, limit participation, or ban contributors when needed to protect
the community. Reports are handled as confidentially as practical, with fair
notice and an opportunity to respond when safety permits.
